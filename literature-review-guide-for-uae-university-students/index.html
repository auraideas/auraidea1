How do I write a literature review for a UAE university?

A practical guide to searching, comparing, and synthesising academic studies instead of writing a disconnected list of summaries.

Request guidance about Literature review writing

Where should you start?

Start with the concepts in your research question and build a search plan with synonyms, databases, dates, and inclusion criteria. Record each useful study with its method, sample, findings, and limitations.

Organise the review around themes, debates, methods, or chronology when justified. Explain what the studies collectively show and where they disagree, then connect the gap to your own research question.

Related terms: literature review UAE, academic sources, research synthesis. Use the phrase that describes your actual question rather than repeating generic keywords.

Quick checklist

  1. Confirm the university and programme requirements.
  2. Match the evidence to the research question.
  3. Document sources, decisions, and limitations.
  4. Review the final work before submission.
