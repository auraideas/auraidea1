How can I prepare my SPSS data for a thesis?

A clear workflow for cleaning, coding, documenting, and analysing SPSS data before presenting results in a master’s or doctoral thesis in the UAE.

Request guidance about SPSS data analysis for thesis students

Where should you start?

Define each variable and coding rule before running tests. Check missing values, duplicates, impossible entries, distributions, and outliers using a documented process that another researcher could understand.

Choose analyses from the research question and measurement level, not from a menu of familiar tests. Report assumptions, effect sizes or confidence intervals where appropriate, and explain limitations alongside p-values.

Related terms: SPSS thesis analysis, data cleaning, statistical results. Use the phrase that describes your actual question rather than repeating generic keywords.

Quick checklist

  1. Confirm the programme and university requirements.
  2. Match the method to the question and evidence.
  3. Document sources, decisions, and limitations.
  4. Review the final document before submission.
