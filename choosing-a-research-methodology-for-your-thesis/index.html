How do I choose a research methodology for my thesis?

A decision framework for matching a quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-methods design to the question, evidence, and practical constraints.

Request guidance about Choosing research methodology

Where should you start?

Define whether you need to measure, compare, explain, explore, or evaluate something. Then consider the population, access, instruments, ethics, time, and type of evidence required by the question.

Do not choose a method because it is familiar or popular. Explain why the design fits the question, how the data will be analysed, and what the design cannot establish.

Related terms: thesis methodology UAE, qualitative quantitative research, research design. Use the phrase that describes your actual question rather than repeating generic keywords.

Quick checklist

  1. Confirm the university and programme requirements.
  2. Match the evidence to the research question.
  3. Document sources, decisions, and limitations.
  4. Review the final work before submission.
