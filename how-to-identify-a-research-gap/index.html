How do I identify a genuine research gap?

A practical process for distinguishing a real gap in knowledge, context, method, or evidence from a topic that is merely interesting.

Request guidance about Identifying a research gap

Where should you start?

Compare recent studies by question, context, population, method, and findings. Look for a specific limitation or unresolved disagreement that matters to your field and can be investigated with available evidence.

A gap is not simply that no one has studied a topic in your city. Explain why the missing evidence matters, what your study can add, and how your design addresses the gap without overstating originality.

Related terms: research gap UAE, thesis topic, academic research problem. Use the phrase that describes your actual question rather than repeating generic keywords.

Quick checklist

  1. Confirm the university and programme requirements.
  2. Match the evidence to the research question.
  3. Document sources, decisions, and limitations.
  4. Review the final work before submission.
